      Questions

      FAQ

      Can't find your answer? Ask in the Discord.

      Is Debox free to play?

      Yes. Everything in the game can be earned by playing. The store is optional and sells ranks, bundles and gamepasses.

      Does real money buy random loot?

      No. Nothing you pay for gives a random reward. Crates and pet eggs are earned in game, by mining, voting and playing.

      Can I play on my phone or console?

      Phones, tablets and Windows PCs on Bedrock can add the server directly: address debox.global, port 8147. Xbox, PlayStation and Switch don't let you add your own servers, so they need a third-party workaround.

      Which versions can join?

      Java 1.21 and newer. On Bedrock, keep the game updated to the latest version.

      Where do I start?

      Join and follow the tutorial. The compass in your last hotbar slot opens /menu, which links to everything else.

      Can I play with friends?

      Yes. Start a gang with /gang, trade with /trade, and buy and sell on the /bazaar and /ah.

      How do I report a player or a bug?

      Use /report in game, or make a ticket in the Discord. Report bugs and exploits privately in a ticket. Abusing them gets you banned.

      I got punished. Can I appeal?

      Yes. Make a ticket in the Discord and explain calmly what happened. Appeals in public chat are ignored.
